Budgetli is a household budgeting app built for Switzerland. You give it a gross salary and a postal code, and it works out what you actually take home after social insurance and federal, cantonal and communal tax. It then sizes a monthly budget against that, tracks what you really spent, and tells you whether your savings goals will land on time.

**Role:** Founder · 2026 – Present

## What I’m building
- Gross-to-net calculation covering AHV/IV/EO, ALV, accident cover, the 2nd pillar, and federal, cantonal and communal tax, with every step shown as a labelled line
- Postal-code-to-canton lookup, so the right tax scale follows from where you live
- Budget buckets split into needs, wants and savings, with yearly bills set aside a twelfth at a time
- Spending ledger and savings goals that reconcile plan against actual and track whether each goal is on time
- Shared household budgets, with costs split by salary and joint or separate tax assessment handled correctly
- End-to-end ownership of the full stack: FastAPI and PostgreSQL backend, React web app, and a native SwiftUI iOS app
