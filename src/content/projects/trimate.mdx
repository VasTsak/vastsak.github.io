TriMate (https://trim8.com/) is an AI-powered web app for triathlete training and race planning.
It combines personalized training plans with computer-vision-based swim-form feedback, helping athletes train smarter and prepare for events with more confidence.

**Role:** Founder · 2024 – Present

## Highlights
<div class="grid-2">
- Mathematical optimization for periodized training plan generation
- Computer vision for swim-form analysis and coaching feedback
- Race planning and pacing tools for triathlons and duathlons
- Built end to end, from early prototype to production deployment with paying users
