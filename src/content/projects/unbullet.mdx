Unbullet is an AI-powered recruiting platform that matches job seekers to opportunities using candidate narrative rather than CV keywords alone. The product is being built around a multi-agent architecture with LLM-driven reasoning to improve fit quality and make talent discovery more meaningful.

## What I’m building
- Multi-agent orchestration for candidate matching and role evaluation
- Prompt iteration and evaluation pipelines for quality and reliability
- End-to-end ownership across product, engineering, and deployment
